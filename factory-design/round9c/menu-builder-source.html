Menu Builder

Lay out every menu screen of Oreheart and animate the intro. Drag pieces to move them, gold corners to size them, the blue dot to turn them. Hold Shift and click to pick several pieces, or drag a box around them; then drag any one to move them all. Press H to hide the handles.
