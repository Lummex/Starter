Game Editor

The whole match screen: your painted arena with its effects, the pieces on the board, and everything on screen. Drag anything to move it; drag a gold corner to size it. Press H to hide every handle.

Screen pieces & text

Everything drawn on top of the board: names, crystals, decks, the hand, buttons. Click a piece on the screen (or in the list) to edit it.

You can also drag a picture file from your computer straight onto the screen.

Pictures (replace any picture)

Every picture on the screen, with the file it uses. Replace one with a file from your computer or a picture on GitHub. "Original" puts the old one back.

Characters & pieces

Stand-in pictures for the 3D pieces, standing on board squares. Drag the white dot at a piece's feet to move it. Sizes are measured against the board square it stands on.

Effects: brightness, size, speed

Turn a whole kind of effect up or down at once (for example, all flames dimmer). To change just one, click its dot on the board.

Foreground cut-outs

Trace around a fence, post or bridge. That piece of the painting is drawn on top, so pieces standing behind it are hidden by it. Click point by point, then click the first point again (or double-click, or press Enter).

Editing: drag the blue squares to move points. Click on the outline to add a point there. Right-click a square to remove that point.

View zoom: 100%

Scroll to zoom the board (the screen pieces stay put, like in the game). Drag with the right mouse button, or hold Space and drag, to slide around.
