# Gnome cards: gold -> silver (Brush, 2026-10-29)

Ken: "wherever there's gold it should be like silver colors. Silver and blue." Each picture is an EDIT of the current card art made in Ken's ChatGPT. Only the gold/brass metal was changed to silver. All 22 are in Oreheart Studio's Art Studio as "Pick one" (artRequests `*-card-silver-*`). The originals are untouched, and nothing has gone into the game.

Notes per picture:
- overclocked-conduit and gyrocopter-ace are made from Brush's earlier edits (two arms and no shield; no helicopter), so each carries both changes.
- cmd-sproingle is the commander card painting. cmd-sproingle-select is the Choose Your Commander portrait (game file art/commanders/sproingle.webp), 1016x1354.
- cmd-sprocket is the commander portrait art/commanders/sprocket.webp, 998x1330. The painter's signature in the corner is gone.
- The other pictures are 1024x1536.
