# 2026-10-29 Brush: gnome card art, gold -> silver (22 pictures)

Job from Forge, Ken's words to Wright: "wherever there's gold it should be like silver colors. Silver and blue."
Each picture is an EDIT of the current card art in Ken's ChatGPT: only the gold/brass metal changed to silver. Brush checked every picture side by side with the original. All 22 are in the Art Studio as "Pick one" (artRequests `*-card-silver-*`). Copies are in factory-design/art-made/gnome-silver/. The originals are untouched, and nothing went into the game.

None that alter the look much. Across the set, the silver makes some pictures a touch cooler or less orange where the gold used to reflect warm light. Unweave and the gyronaut had their brown leather turn grey on the first try; both were redone.
