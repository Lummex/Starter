## What was built
- **PvP friend matches by 6-letter code.** The server (Supabase Edge Function `match`) holds the only full match state, rolls every die and shuffle, checks every move (turn, move number, legality) and sends each player only their own view: the other hand and both decks are card backs, the random seed is gone, and the second player's board is mirrored so their browser always plays as "player". Ken's clock lives on the server: 2 minutes a phase, unused time banked up to 60 s, a 12 s Respond window that freezes the main clock (and in PvP the window ALWAYS opens, so nobody can tell who holds a counter), auto-Done on timeout, three timeouts in a row = forfeit, Give up = forfeit. Reconnect: closing the tab and coming back (or "Back to your match" on the menu) returns to the same match. Standing orders, chase, guard arrows and stances still work (they send ordinary moves). Vs-computer play is unchanged.
- **Coins and packs (server side):** four packs (Oreheart, Junkpile, Gizmo, Deepforge), 8 cards for 100 coins, odds shown to the player (5 Common, 2 Uncommon, 1 Rare 85 % / Ultra Rare 13 % / Collector 2 %), rolled on the server, one charge per request even if repeated, coins can never go below zero, a collection per player (every card is an instance with its history), decks may only use cards the player owns (everyone owns the starter collection), all numbers admin-editable (settings).
- **Daily quests:** 3 a day (one easy 25, one medium 50, one hard 75 coins), one free reroll a day, unfinished quests stay 3 days, a free pack every 7 days of finishing all three. Names in the game's voice ("Pick a Fight", "Summon the Rabble", "Clink Clink", "THWACK!", "Winner Winner", "Big Spender", "Bonk Collector", "Shield Wall", "Crown Jewels", "Mine, All Mine", "Right in the Kisser", "Hat Trick" ...). Rewards are paid exactly once. PvP progress is counted by the server from the match itself; vs-computer matches are reported by the browser with daily caps (20 reports a day, per-match caps).
- **Pack opening:** Brush's painted foil packs (the general one plus a faction pack each); the pack shines and wobbles, shakes, glows, RIPS along a torn top edge (the strip spins off), foil shreds fly, 8 cards slide out face down, the player flips each (or Flip all), rarer cards glow more (silver rim, blue-white bloom, violet bloom with sparkles, rainbow for Collector; no gold), NEW tags, rip and flip sounds. Smooth on PC and phone (transforms/opacity only).
- **Menu:** signed-in players get a coin chip, Shop, Collection, Quests and "Play a friend"; the Deck Builder shows "owned ×N" and greys out unowned cards.
