## What was built
Oreheart, a cute cartoony card-battle game for phones, built as an installable app that works offline. It has a main menu, a 7×7 board match against a computer opponent (Easy/Normal/Hard), and the 80-card set. It also has a Collection, a Deck Builder, a booster-pack Shop, a How to Play tutorial and Settings. Progress is saved on the device.

## Try these things
- On your phone, open the preview and tap Play, then Start Match. Check the coin flip and the "Your Starting Hand" screen, then keep your hand.
- Tap a card in your hand, then a highlighted tile in your bottom two rows to summon it. Press Undo to take it back.
- End your turn and watch the enemy play. It should finish in about 5 seconds.
- Wait a turn, then tap your creature to see blue move tiles and red attack tiles. Press Hint for a suggestion.
- Open Shop and buy a booster pack (50 gold, which you start with). Tap the cards to flip them.
- Close the app mid-match and reopen it. "Continue" should appear on the menu.
