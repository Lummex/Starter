## What was built
This round gives Oreheart's match screen a painted swamp arena ("Skullfang") as the battlefield, replacing the old plain 3D floor. Squares line up with the painting, and the scenery is animated (flames, waterfalls, fireflies). You can zoom and pan the view, and a Recenter button resets it. Crystal mines now use a 3D crystal model.

## Try these things
- Start a match (Play, pick a Commander and deck, Fight!) and look at the painted arena and its animations.
- Zoom in and out with the mouse wheel. Pan with the arrow keys, WASD, or by dragging empty board.
- Press Recenter, or double-click the board, to reset the view.
- Drag a card from your hand onto the board and check that the highlighted squares sit on the painting.
- Drag a creature to move or attack. The view shouldn't pan while you do.
- Resize the window, or try a phone held sideways, and check that no black bars appear.
