## What was built
Oreheart, a fantasy card battle in the browser. You play Goblins, Gnomes or Dwarves against a computer opponent (Easy or Normal) on a 13×7 board with three random crystal mines. There is also a guided tutorial. This round (revision 7) switched to one crystal type (Flow and Bank). It also added Miner upgrades, a "Mine All" button, spell callouts, card reveals, full card text and new card names.

## Try these things
- Start the Tutorial and follow the tips. It now includes a "Mine All" step and a Miner-upgrade step.
- Start a match and drag a Miner next to a mine, then press "⛏️ Mine All" (or the M key) during your Action phase.
- Drag a Miner card onto one of your own creatures. It should power that creature up at once, with no pop-up.
- Hover over a hand card. It should grow to double size and stay on screen.
- Cast a spell and look for the comic text over the caster's Stronghold, such as "GRUBNAK CASTS INFERNAL ORB!".
- Open Settings and switch "Card reveals" between Normal and Fast.
