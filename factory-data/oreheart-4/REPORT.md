## What was built
OreHeart is a dark-fantasy card game for phones that runs in the browser against a computer opponent. It has a main menu, battles on a three-Mine board, an 80-card collection, a deck builder, a booster-pack shop, a How to Play guide and settings. It can be installed to a phone's home screen and works offline. Progress is saved on the device.

## Try these things
- On the main menu, check that Gold shows 300, then open every screen and use Back.
- Play → Start Battle (Normal). Tap a card in your hand, tap a slot to place it, then tap End Turn and watch the computer take its turn.
- During a battle, open Menu → Concede → Concede. You should see Defeat and no gold.
- Collection: set Faction to Fire (should show 20 of 80), then add Legendary (should show 2 of 80).
- Shop: buy a booster pack, tap Reveal All, press Done, and check gold dropped to 200. Reload to confirm it stays.
- Open it on a real phone, in portrait, and check that the board is readable and the buttons are easy to tap.

## Things to know
- I only had two screenshots, both of the main menu (desktop and phone). They look clean and correctly styled: gold heart emblem, gold headings, no cramping, no sideways scrolling. I did not see the battle board, collection, or shop pictures, so I can't vouch for how they look.
- The builder says the three slots in each Mine are only about 38px wide on a phone, which is narrower than the 44px tap-size goal. Heights are fine. Tapping them accurately on a real phone is worth testing.
- The builder says the computer's turn is capped at about 8 seconds.
- Battles are not saved. Reloading mid-battle abandons the game with no penalty.
- How well the computer plays, and card balance, are not covered by the automated checks. Play a few games to judge them.
